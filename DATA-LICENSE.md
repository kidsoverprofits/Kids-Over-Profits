## What this covers

- The facility, operator, referrer, transporter and provider records
- The network map data (`js/data/network/`)
- The TTI glossary (`js/data/glossary/`)
- The reporting directory (`js/data/reporting/`)
- The inspection-report indexes and severe-finding summaries Kids Over Profits
  wrote
- The lawsuit, legislation and news indexes (titles, dates, parties, links and
  our summaries)
- The bulk downloads on the site's open data page, which carry the same license

## What this does not cover

These belong to their authors or are public records, so their own terms apply:

- News articles, court filings and other third-party documents, including the
  copies kept in the site's media library
- State inspection reports and other government records
- Photos and logos
