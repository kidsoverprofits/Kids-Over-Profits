## 2. Owner: runs and settings only the owner can do

### 2.6 App and extension

1. **Try the app on a phone** (Expo Go, `npx expo start` in `kids-over-profits-mobile`): a facility with
   incidents and staff, the Map tab (search, Back, the drawer, Key/List/Timeline), Send (each kind once, check
   it reaches the Review inbox), Donate, a search for "Copper Canyon".
   Ask: Try the app on your phone, one screen at a time; note what breaks
   Why: Nobody has used the app on a real phone yet.
   Kind: check
   Time: about 5 min a step
   Step: Start it: `npx expo start` in `kids-over-profits-mobile`, open it in Expo Go
   Step: Open a facility with incidents and staff
   Step: The Map tab: search, Back, the drawer, Key / List / Timeline
   Step: Send one of each kind, then check each reached the Review inbox
   Step: Donate
   Step: Search for "Copper Canyon"
2. **Artwork and listing**: icon, splash, adaptive icon; short and long descriptions, screenshots, age rating.
   Ask: Who makes the app's icon, splash screen and store listing?
   Why: The stores won't take the app without them.
   Choices: Me | The design volunteer | Claude drafts the text, a person makes the art
   Time: 1 min
- **Privacy policy and the app**: check `/privacy-policy/` covers the app.
  Ask: Does the privacy policy cover the app? (the stores ask)
  Why: Both stores require a policy that covers what the app collects.
  Go: [/privacy-policy/](https://kidsoverprofits.org/privacy-policy/)
  Choices: It covers it | It doesn't: Claude drafts an app section
  Time: 5 min
3. **Site links that open the app**: after the first real build, the Apple Team ID and the Android signing
   SHA-256, for a session to ship the two `/.well-known/` files.
   Ask: After the first app build: paste the Apple Team ID and the Android signing SHA-256
   Why: Links to kidsoverprofits.org then open in the app.
   Time: 5 min, after the store accounts (below)
4. **Send to KOP extension**: load it unpacked and send one page; publish
   `dist/send-to-kop-<version>.zip` to the Chrome Web Store (5 USD) and Firefox add-ons; then a session puts the
   store links on `/send/`. Safari needs a Mac with Xcode.
   Ask: Try the Send to KOP browser extension: load it and send one page
   Why: Check it works before anyone else installs it.
   Kind: check
   Time: 10 min
   Step: chrome://extensions > Developer mode > "Load unpacked", pick the kop-send-to-kop folder
   Step: Send one page with it, then find it in the Review inbox
- **Extension in the stores**: publish `dist/send-to-kop-<version>.zip` to the Chrome Web Store (5 USD) and Firefox
  add-ons; then a session puts the store links on `/send/`.
  Ask: Put the extension on the Chrome (5 USD once) and Firefox stores?
  Why: Volunteers can then install it in one click instead of loading it by hand.
  Choices: Yes, both | Chrome only | Not yet
  Time: 1 min to decide
5. **Store accounts as the nonprofit (2026-10-26)**: KOP is in the IRS master file as a 501(c)(3)
   (EIN 99-4823769, PO Box 893, Bath ME 04530-0893, ruling 2025-01). Apple waives the 99 USD a year for
   recognized nonprofits that sell no digital goods (the app sells none; Donate links out to /donate/). Google Play
   has no waiver: 25 USD once. Steps, all the owner's (legal attestation, Apple and Google sign-in):
   a) a D-U-N-S number for Kids Over Profits, free through Apple's lookup at
      https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/ (D&B wants a street address, not the PO box; up to
      about 5 business days); b) enroll at https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/ as an Organization,
      "Nonprofit", tick the fee waiver, with an Apple Account on a @kidsoverprofits.org address, 2FA on, and
      the IRS determination letter ready (Apple phones to check authority); c) Play Console
      https://play.google.com/console/signup as an Organization with the same D-U-N-S (organization accounts skip
      the 12-tester, 14-day closed test that personal accounts need). Each renewal the Apple Account Holder
      confirms eligibility again. Then item 3 (Team ID, SHA-256).
   Ask: Set up the Apple and Google developer accounts as the nonprofit
   Why: The app can't ship without them; Apple waives its 99 USD fee for nonprofits.
   Time: 15 min each, plus up to 5 business days waiting for the D-U-N-S number
   Step: [Request a free D-U-N-S number](https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/) (needs a street address, not the PO box)
   Step: Pick the @kidsoverprofits.org address that will own the accounts, and turn on 2FA for it
   Step: [Enroll with Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) as an Organization, "Nonprofit", tick the fee waiver (have the IRS letter ready)
   Step: [Sign up for Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/signup) as an Organization with the same D-U-N-S (25 USD)
