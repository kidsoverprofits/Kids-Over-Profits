# Database tables

What each table holds and which file owns it. A new table goes here (and in `DATABASE-COLUMNS.md`).

- `facilities_v2` (+ `{prefix}kop_operators`, `kop_operator_facilities`, `kop_facility_locations`) - Facility records, one JSON document per facility (`docs/FACILITY-SCHEMA.md`); `facilities_master` is the frozen legacy copy
- `suggested_edits` - Public submissions pending approval
- `locations_master` / `referrers_master` - Related data
- `providers_master` - Mental health providers outside the TTI (psychiatric wards, PHP/IOP, day schools, respite, outpatient) that use TTI practices or refer to TTI facilities; the data form's "providers" category (`js/data-form/provider-form.js`), kept out of the facility tables
- `marketers_master` - Marketing companies that sell programs to parents (websites, "independent" directories, help lines, sold inquiries, reputation work, instructions for AI chatbots): the data forms' Marketers tab (`js/data-form/marketer-form.js`, `templates/data-form-marketer.php`), stored as `{marketerCompany}` only (`api/lib-marketers.php`), listed on /marketing-companies/ (`inc/marketers.php`); seeded once per version from `seeds/marketers.json` (adds missing records, never changes one)
- `tradeorgs_master` - Trade organizations (NATSAP, the OBH Council, IECA, ISPA, NATC, NIPSA, SBSA, YATA, AMATS, AEE, EAGALA): the data forms' Trade Organizations tab (`js/data-form/tradeorg-form.js`, `templates/data-form-tradeorg.php`), stored as `{tradeOrg}` only (`api/lib-tradeorgs.php`), listed on /trade-organizations/ (`inc/tradeorgs.php`) with the programs whose records name it under memberships/accreditations (`kop_tradeorg_membership_match()`: name, acronym or other name; "former" in the entry = former member), and transport companies whose records list it among affiliations (`kop_tradeorgs_transport_members()`); seeded from `seeds/tradeorgs.json` like marketers. Record kind `tradeorg` in Woodbury, wiki links, the finder and People
- `wiki_submissions` / `news_submissions` - Content submissions
- `{prefix}kop_volunteers` / `{prefix}kop_volunteer_recs` - Volunteer reviewers (name, hash of their link's token) and their approve/reject/not sure recommendations on review inbox items, closed with the admin's decision (`inc/review-volunteers.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_vol_practice` - Practice rounds: items an admin already decided (drawn from `kop_review_log`), the admin's answer and the volunteer's call; never recommendations, never in agreement rates (`inc/volunteer-practice.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_person_jobs` - A person's past jobs (place, role, years, source), held once and shown on every page that lists them; read in from staff entries' pastJobs by the hourly sync, edited at KOP Tools > People (`inc/person-jobs.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_person_relations` - Relationships between people (family, business partners...): the other person's id or name, kind, detail, source; shown on both people at KOP Tools > People, printed under each person on facility and company pages and drawn on the network map between two people on it (`inc/person-relations.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_people` / `{prefix}kop_person_roles` - One id per person named anywhere in the records (`personId` on each `facilities_v2` staff entry; everyone else linked by name), its pool (industry, harmed, press), and where each is named (derived, rebuilt by the hourly sync; `inc/people.php`)
- `journalists` / `journalist_articles` - Internal-only list of journalists covering the TTI, extracted from news bylines (`api/lib-journalists.php`, managed in `api/manage-journalists.php`); never exposed publicly
- `inspection_report_counts` - Each inspection report's state verdicts (citations, high-risk, repeat, complaints, substantiated) as JSON, its year and a content hash so a report stored twice counts once; filled hourly (`inc/inspection-rollup.php`)
- `facility_closure_reports` / `news_closure_scans` - Closures the hourly news scan found (one row per article and program, pending until an admin confirms) and which articles it has read (`inc/closure-reports.php`)
- `news_facility_candidates` / `news_facility_scans` - Every facility name from the news with no record and what the scan decided (created, matched, possible duplicate, provider, not a facility), one row per name, and which articles it has read (`inc/facility-discovery.php`)
- `indigenous_schools` / `indigenous_school_news` - Indian boarding, residential and mission schools, kept out of the facility tables (never on facility pages, hubs, map, search or open data), and which articles are about each (school_id 0 = the schools in general); the news scan files a school it finds as `review = 'pending'` (`inc/indigenous-schools.php`)
- `young_adult_programs` - Programs for people 18 and older, connected to the industry but kept in their own table, out of the facility tables; `facts` is a JSON list of Woodbury items, each citing its issue page (`inc/young-adult-programs.php`; `ya` on `{prefix}kop_woodbury_facts` marks a no-record program's items for its tab); `document_folder_id` is its media library folder, shown on its card (`kop_ya_documents_html()`)
- `lawsuit_facility_links` / `lawsuit_news_links` - Which facilities a lawsuit involves and which articles cover it (synced on save; `api/lawsuit-facility-links.php`, `api/lawsuit-news-links.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_woodbury_mentions` - Woodbury Reports pages about a program (article, news item or mention) found by `scripts/woodbury-scan.py`, pending until an admin files them in the program's "Woodbury Reports Mentions" folder
- `{prefix}kop_media_folder_tags` - Extra folder memberships (one document, many folders)
- `{prefix}kop_doc_types` / `{prefix}kop_doc_type_suggestions` - What kind of document each attachment is (one row per type, with rule / ai / person / checked) and, for documents the rules could not type, the AI's guess and its review status (`inc/doc-types.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_podcast_items` - On-air statements and facts read from the industry's podcasts (quote, second, speaker, the whole reader entry), pending until the owner approves one linked to a person id and records (`records`: "123,company:45") or rejects it; admins only (`inc/podcast-statements.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_program_practices` - One line per rule, level, punishment or limit read from a program's own handbook (its document, page, quote, topic, the checklist boxes it shows), pending until the owner adds it to the facility page or leaves it off (`inc/program-practices.php`); rows with `answer_id` are readers' answers on /questions/ read into lines (`inc/reader-accounts.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_nonprofit_links` - Which nonprofit's IRS returns (Form 990, files in `js/data/nonprofit-filings/orgs/`) show on a facility or company page (`inc/nonprofit-filings.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_business_links` - Which state-registered company (state + document number; files in `js/data/business-filings/<st>/entities/`) shows on a facility or company page (`inc/business-filings.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_directory_edits` - Every change made at KOP Tools > Directory Editor (company edits, hide/show, delete, merge, program links and moves) with the rows before and after, for exact Undo (`inc/directory-editor.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_investors` / `{prefix}kop_investor_links` / `{prefix}kop_investor_log` - Investors (private equity firms, lenders, royalty funds...), their stakes in companies or programs (role, first and last year, the stake's own words, source), and every change with the rows before and after for exact Undo (`inc/investors.php`, KOP Tools > Investors)
- `{prefix}kop_campuses` / `{prefix}kop_campus_programs` - Separate programs that share one campus (a hospital's two residential programs), one campus per record (`inc/campus-programs.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_folder_links` - Legacy/current-name folder equivalence (curated in `api/link-folders.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_glossary_nodes` / `_entries` / `_aliases` / `_tags` / `_log` - The TTI glossary: sections and groups, terms, other names, program tags, and each entry change for Undo (`inc/glossary-store.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_glossary_feedback` - Reader notes from the glossary's "My facility used this too" / "Suggest a correction" buttons (`inc/glossary-feedback.php`; reviewed under KOP Tools > Glossary Feedback)
- `{prefix}kop_question_answers` / `{prefix}kop_question_state` / `{prefix}kop_question_batches` - Readers' answers on /questions/, one per person and question; each settled or dismissed question with what it wrote for Undo; and the sets of answers whose reader asked for one email when they are checked (`inc/questions.php`; Review inbox > Answers to our questions). An answer's `read_status` / `read_json` hold its reading into lines, what is not published and its leads (`inc/reader-accounts.php`)
- `{prefix}kop_addresses` / `{prefix}kop_facility_addresses` - Physical address IDs and which facility stood where (`api/manage-addresses.php`; join table rebuilt from facility data on each seed)
