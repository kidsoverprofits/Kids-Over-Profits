Academy at Swift River "Program Facts" page (captured Dec. 31, 2008), published under Aspen Education Group.
ASR Fast Facts: Established in 1997 in Cummington, MA; 100 students, 100% boarding; college preparatory / therapeutic; year-round rolling admissions; grades 9-12, co-educational; flexible length of stay, average 15 months; 636 acre campus; ages 14-17.5 (average 16.5); representing 28 states; 30% females / 70% males.
"Pathways" orientation: transition from home, RTC or wilderness programs; small orientation group (average 12); academics begin at completion of Pathways; specialized RTC transition track.
Clinical: "Life Phases Clinical Model"; individual counseling; group sessions; CADAC substance abuse specialist; "Working Visit" 4-6 weeks after enrollment; "Psychiatric support with medication consultation and management available."
Team model: 5 teams, each with 2-3 clinical counselors, 2 teachers, 3 residential mentors, a supervisor. 10 teachers.
