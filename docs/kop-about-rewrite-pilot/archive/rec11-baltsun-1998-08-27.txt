Baltimore Sun (wire, Phoenix dateline), "Arizona denies license to 'boot camp' after death," Aug. 27, 1998.
- Five-month DES investigation concluded "abuse and neglect" led to Nicholaus Contreraz's death and DES denied the ranch an operating license; 17 former staff to be placed on the Arizona Child Abuser Directory.
- DES Director Linda Blessing: the death and "the repeated mistreatment of other residents, demonstrate a pattern of abuse and neglect ... and a lack of concern by senior management and line staff."
- Officials could not recall the state ever denying such a license; 20 days to appeal. The institution was 49 years old.
- FBI civil rights investigation and Pinal County criminal review ongoing.
- July 1998: California DSS advised counties it was ending funding for out-of-state placements at the ranch; nearly three-fourths of the 400 juveniles at its seven campuses were from California. California report found "medical neglect and physical abuse" and "widespread excessive use of physical restraint and hands-on confrontation."
