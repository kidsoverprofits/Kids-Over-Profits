Daily Hampshire Gazette, Fran Ryan, "Academy at Swift River, therapeutic school for teens, to close at summer's end," last modified Wednesday, July 24, 2013.
CUMMINGTON — After serving as a therapeutic college preparatory facility for teens since 1997, the Academy at Swift River will soon close.
Executive director Bar Clarke, in a statement: the academy "will cease operations at the end of the summer."
The 636 acre co-educational campus houses 55 to 65 students in grades 9 through 12. Typical length of stay 15 months.
Spokeswoman Kristen Hayes: staff cutbacks already begun; roughly 50 employees will lose their jobs.
Clarke's statement cited changing market dynamics and the inability of many families to finance treatment.
Before the school, the property was the Swift River Inn (restaurant, suites, banquet hall, converted barn).
"The Academy at Swift River is a program of Aspen Education Group, headquartered in Cerritos, Calif."
