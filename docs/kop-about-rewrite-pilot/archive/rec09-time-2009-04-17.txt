TIME, Maia Szalavitz, "An Oregon School for Troubled Teens Is Under Scrutiny," April 2009 (captured Apr. 18, 2009).
- Mount Bachelor Academy, near Prineville, "is under state investigation for allegations of abuse reported by students and one employee"; Oregon DHS declined details of the ongoing investigations, "which include a second inquiry based on possible licensing violations."
- Per 10 students, two separate parents and a part-time employee interviewed by TIME: in required seminars the school calls Lifesteps, staff instructed girls, some of whom say they were victims of rape or sexual abuse, to dress in provocative clothing (fishnet stockings, high heels, miniskirts) and perform lap dances for male students as therapy.
- Executive director Sharon Bitz denied the charges: reports are "inaccurate representations ... Some of the accusations are demonstrably false, while others have been exaggerated for shock effect." Told the Bend Bulletin: "We would never ask a student to give a lap dance."
- The school was the subject of Forest Grove v. T.A. (U.S. Supreme Court argument April 28, 2009).
- Mount Bachelor "houses up to 125 students, charges $6,400 per month"; Aspen Education Group 2008 revenue topped $132 million.
- NOTE: this article does NOT state that DHS cleared the school after 1998 (the Wikipedia claim cited to it).
