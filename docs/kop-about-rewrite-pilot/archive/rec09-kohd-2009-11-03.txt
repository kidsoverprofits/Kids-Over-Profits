KOHD, Rachel Azevedo, "Mount Bachelor Academy Ordered to Temporarily Close," Nov. 3, 2009.
- Seven-month Oregon DHS investigation "finds nine substantited findings of child abuse"; state ordered temporary closure of the therapeutic boarding school 26 miles east of Prineville.
- DHS spokesman Gene Evans: "We are asking parents to begin making arrangements for their children to leave."
- Investigation began after complaints in March 2009; about 90 students aged 14-17 at the time; licensed as a therapeutic boarding school.
- Allegations initially came from five students and focused on the mandatory "lifesteps" program; activities required students to reenact past traumatic events, including prior physical or sexual abuse; DHS found all areas of instruction "created an immediate threat to all students."
- School statement: "We vigorously disagree with the state's findings"; would seek a hearing and possibly a TRO; 90 days to implement new guidelines in a dozen areas.
