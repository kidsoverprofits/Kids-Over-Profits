The Bulletin (Bend), Claire Withycombe, "Mount Bachelor academy suits resolved privately; Three suits sought over $48 million, alleged sexual abuse and mistreatment," Jan. 2015.
- Three lawsuits filed in Crook County Circuit Court against Mount Bachelor Academy and affiliated parties "have been resolved privately," per plaintiffs' attorney Maria "Sam" Ruckwardt (O'Connell Clark & Crew); confidentiality prevents disclosing compensation.
- "In total, 51 former students alleged mistreatment ..., seeking over $48 million in damages from the Mount Bachelor Academy, its defunct owner, Aspen Educational Group, and CRC Health Group and CRC Health Oregon."
- "The first suit was filed in 2011 in Multnomah County while the second and third were filed in 2012. All three were subsequently moved to Crook County."
- Two cases dismissed with prejudice Nov. 17 [2014], the third Sept. 16 [2014]. Feb. 6, 2014 letter to Judge Daniel J. Ahern reported an agreement to mediate.
- November 2009 DHS report: abuse allegations founded; students subject to sexualized role-play in front of peers and adults, sleep deprivation, extended "self studies" with strenuous work projects and camping alone in inclement weather; compelled to re-enact traumatic experiences of abuse; inadequate mental health services; practices violated its therapeutic boarding school license.
- January 2013: Ahern ruled one case could proceed despite a statute-of-limitations defense; in 2009 Oregon raised the age limit for child sexual abuse suits from 24 to 40.
