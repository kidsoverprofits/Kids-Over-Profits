The Bulletin, Sheila G. Miller, "Lawsuit against boarding school to be tried in Prineville," Nov. 10, 2011.
- First suit: nine former students, filed July [2011] in Multnomah County Circuit Court; eight more added in August; seeks $25.5 million. Judge ruled it is a Central Oregon case to be tried in Prineville.
- "On Wednesday afternoon, Clark brought a second lawsuit against the school" for 14 more former students, seeking $23 million. Clark cites 31 people suing.
- Defendants: Mount Bachelor Educational Center, Aspen Education Group, CRC Health Group, CRC Health Oregon.
