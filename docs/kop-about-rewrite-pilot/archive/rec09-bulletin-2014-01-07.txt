The Bulletin, Shelby R. King, "Lawsuit against Mount Bachelor Academy moving forward," Jan. 7, 2014.
- "Kelly Clark filed three lawsuits in 2011" (Clark died Dec. 2013; O'Donnell Clark & Crew's Maria Ruckwardt and Steve Crew took over).
- Three cases pending, to be tried in Crook County Circuit Court absent settlement; second and third stayed until the first concludes.
- First: 17 former students, $26 million (school, Aspen Education Group, CRC Health Oregon). Second: 15 former students, $22.85 million. Third: 13 former students, $19.5 million.
