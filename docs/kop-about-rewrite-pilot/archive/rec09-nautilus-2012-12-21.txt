Nautilus Insurance Corp. v. Mount Bachelor Educational Center, Inc., et al., No. 3:12-cv-00825-SI (D. Or.), Opinion and Order, Dkt. 54, Dec. 21, 2012 (Simon, J.). Insurer seeks a declaration of no duty to defend/indemnify in the state actions; stay granted, transfer denied.
- "In 2011 and 2012, the former students filed three actions in Oregon state courts."
- (1) MLM v. Mount Bachelor Educational Center, Inc., Case No. 1107-08552, Multnomah County, "probably in 2011"; (2) LF v. Mount Bachelor Educational Center, Inc., Case No. 1109-11909, Multnomah County, Sept. 13, 2011; (3) Doe 210 v. Mount Bachelor Educational Center, Inc., No. 12-cv-0008, Deschutes County, Jan. 4, 2012. "The LF and DOE Actions were apparently later transferred to Crook County."
- Former-student defendants: MLM, NAA, TGB, RB, MST, TL, BPO, NCQ, WBL, LF, Does 110-117, Does 210-223; residents between 1990 and 2008.
