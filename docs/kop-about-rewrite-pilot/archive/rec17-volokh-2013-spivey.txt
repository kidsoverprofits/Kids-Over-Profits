The Volokh Conspiracy (Eugene Volokh), "Court Enforces Religious Arbitration Agreement, Over Objection of Plaintiff," Oct. 15, 2013.
- Case: Spivey v. Teen Challenge Inc. (Fla. Ct. App. [1st DCA] Oct. 11, 2013). Pamela Spivey's son, Nicklaus Ellison, then age 19, "enrolled in a year-long program at Teen Challenge's substance abuse facility."
- Ellison signed an arbitration agreement providing for arbitration by Christian conciliation; under Florida law the wrongful-death plaintiff "stands in the shoes" of the decedent and is bound by it.
- Spivey objected to provisions providing for prayer in the process; the court enforced the agreement.
