The Oregonian / OregonLive, Amy Hsuan, "Mount Bachelor Academy in Prineville to close by Dec. 9," Nov. 9, 2009.
- Will close by December 9; loss of 69 to 72 jobs.
- DHS told parents to remove their children the previous week "after a seven-month investigation concluded that students were subject to inappropriate sexual role-play, public humiliation and physical deprivation." The state gave the academy 90 days to correct violations (DHS spokesman Gene Evans).
- Parent company Aspen Education Group announced permanent closure in a WARN notice; SVP Mark Dorenfeld cited "the revocation of the Facility's operating license," but Evans said the state had only temporarily suspended the license.
- As of March 2009 the school enrolled 88 students and employed 77 staff; ages 14 to 17 1/2; tuition $6,400 a month; typical stay 14 to 16 months.
