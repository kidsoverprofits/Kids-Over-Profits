Seven Days, "Judge Moves Vermont Family's Wrongful Death Lawsuit to Religious Proceeding" (2023).
- A Florida judge ruled the Wood family can't pursue a wrongful death lawsuit against the faith-based organization that ran the school where Naomi Wood, 17, died in May 2020.
- Judge Michael McDaniel ordered the family to resolve the conflict with Adult & Teen Challenge USA "through Christian arbitration, an alternative to litigation governed by Biblical principles and presided over by a Christian mediator, not a judge."
- The family called on the Polk County Sheriff's Office and Florida to reopen the investigation.
- Adult & Teen Challenge is affiliated with the Pentecostal Assemblies of God.
