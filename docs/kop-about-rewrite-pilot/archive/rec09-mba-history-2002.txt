Mount Bachelor Academy "MBA History" (school's own site, captured Feb. 4, 2002).
- 1987: educator Linda Houghton approached by Barry Weiss of College Health Enterprises (CHE); founding staff included Dennis Crowell (Academy Director), Jeannie Crowell, Jane Stewart, Steve Houghton, Alex Bitz. Permit difficulties in Powell Butte; built at Mark's Creek Lodge, Ochoco National Forest. Executive Director Linda Houghton.
- First emotional growth workshops "Lifesteps"; first graduating class December 1990.
- Tim Brace, Executive Director, joined 1993, led until 1997. Sharon Bitz became Executive Director in May 1997 after 8 years at MBA.
- 1998: CHE (President Elliot Sainer) formed Aspen Youth Services, later renamed Aspen Education Group.
- Address: 33051 N.E. Ochoco Hwy., Prineville, OR 97754.
