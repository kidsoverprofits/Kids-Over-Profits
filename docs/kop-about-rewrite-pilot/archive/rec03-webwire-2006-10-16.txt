CAICA press release via WebWire, Oct. 16, 2006 (advocacy release; allegations, not findings).
- Aug. 25, 2006: William Chase Wood and parents Tammy and Gregory Wood sued WWASPS and associates (Wood v. WWASPS).
- Twenty-five new plaintiffs joined; Amended Complaint filed in Utah federal court Oct. 13, 2006; plaintiffs from CA, TX, MD, KY, LA, FL, MN, MI; Turley Law Firm (Dallas).
- Alleged abuse listed in the amended complaint includes forced eating of vomit, beatings, binding, isolation, denial of food and medical care, sexual abuse, forced labor, strip searches.
- Defendants include WWASPS, Cross Creek Center for Boys LLC, Cross Creek Manor LLC, Robert B. Lichfield, Ken Kay, Karr Farnsworth, and others.
- Supports the October 2006 plaintiff count (25 new plaintiffs, 26+ total with the Wood family) — cite the amended complaint where possible; the release is advocacy material.
