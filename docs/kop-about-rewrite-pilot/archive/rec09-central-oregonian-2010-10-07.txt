Central Oregonian (Prineville), Jason Chaney, "Mount Bachelor Academy, DHS reach settlement — Oregon Department of Human Services agreed to dismiss or modify abuse findings, school will stay closed," October 07, 2010.
- Last November [2009] DHS completed a seven-month investigation, "finding several substantiated incidents of abuse," ordered MBA to suspend therapeutic and residential services, giving 90 days to correct; violations were not corrected and the school remains closed.
- Settlement recitals: a month after the order MBA requested a hearing; March 2010 agreement on the orders; four legal proceedings by MBA and executive director Sharon Bitz Dec. 2009-July 2010 (Bitz sued DHS Dec. 2009; April 2010 notices of claim to the Department of Administrative Services; July 2010 MBA lawsuit contesting the abuse findings).
- Findings modified: "substantiated" replaced with "Based on the evidence available to the Department (DHS), the Department found that there is reasonable cause to believe that abuse or neglect occurred. The Department's conclusion is based on evidence collected solely by the Department as of Nov. 2, 2009 ..."
- DHS (Erinn Kelley-Siel): "MBA acknowledged that based on the evidence available to DHS on Nov. 2, 2009, the agency had reasonable cause to believe that abuse or neglect occurred ... MBA agrees that DHS had a reasonable basis to investigate."
- Aspen Education Group President Phil Herschman claimed the withdrawal of the suspension order showed DHS lacked justification.
- DHS will withdraw substantiated findings against Bitz on condition she completes a DHS-approved two-year course in child/adolescent psychology; otherwise findings will be entered.
- School "by all accounts, will remain closed"; Herschman hoped to open a new school on the campus.
