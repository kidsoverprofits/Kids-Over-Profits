Now

On July 1, 2006, New Haven (Spanish Fork, UT) bought Sunrise RTC, "which makes it part of Solacium Holdings, LLC. Solacium also owns Alldredge Academy in West Virginia". In April 2007, Wellcore, LLC (Anthony Geraci, Sugar Land, TX; Fulshear Ranch Academy) merged with Solacium Holdings. In August 2007, Solacium, then of Missouri City, TX, "owner and operator of behavioral healthcare facilities, programs, and schools", signed a letter of intent to buy Wilderness Quest (Monticello, UT); CEO Val H. Christensen announced Alldredge Academy would become Alldredge Wilderness Journey. Sommerville Young Adult Community was listed as "a part of Solacium Holdings LLC" (Woodbury Reports, October 2007, pp. 21 and 27: https://kidsoverprofits.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/woodbury-1007.pdf#page=21; https://kidsoverprofits.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/woodbury-1007.pdf#page=27). Whether the Wilderness Quest purchase closed is not on record: the December 2008 list of programs leaves it out. On April 30, 2008, Anthony Geraci resigned as COO and CMO; David Michel succeeded Val Christensen as President and CEO. In June 2008, Solacium was "majority owned by CIC Partners of Dallas", formed about two years earlier from Fulshear Ranch and New Haven, and managed five programs. On November 3, 2008, "SOLACIUM NOW INNERCHANGE": CEO David Michel announced the renaming; programs New Haven, Sunrise, Fulshear Ranch Academy and Alldredge Wilderness Journey.

In 2009 it offices listed in Addison, TX (Woodbury Reports, April 2009, p. 17: https://kidsoverprofits.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/woodbury-0409.pdf#page=17); later Orem, UT. In December 2010, Dustin Tibbitts, Executive Director of New Haven, promoted to President of InnerChange. In December 2011, Cressey & Company bought InnerChange from CIC Partners. On January 2, 2013, InnerChange bought Chrysalis (Eureka, MT), "the fourth facility to join the InnerChange family of treatment programs", all for adolescent girls or young women. In August 2013, "InnerChange, a clinical and operational consulting organization supporting residential treatment centers", announced a partnership with the Oakley School (Oakley, UT; closed 2017); Paul Taylor, from Copper Canyon Academy, became Oakley's executive director. About 2014: InnerChange bought Lake House Academy (Flat Rock, NC).

On October 1, 2015, Vive Family Support Program joined: "As of October 1st, Vive Family Support Program has joined the InnerChange 'One Family.'". In March 2016, "Optimum Performance Institute, or OPI, has officially joined the InnerChange 'One Family' of programs", a residential treatment center for young men and women 17-28 in Southern California. In August 2018, Calo Programs bought Dragonfly Transitions, a program for young adults in Oregon, from Glen White and Mona Treadway (Herald and News, August 5, 2018: https://www.heraldandnews.com/news/local_news/community/dragonfly-transitions-under-new-ownership/article_f615c677-6bc1-51b3-bb0b-a1d89c32c3e1.html); by the October 31, 2018 Embark announcement it was listed under InnerChange. On October 31, 2018, Embark Behavioral Health was announced as the umbrella name for Calo Programs, InnerChange Programs, New Vision Wilderness Programs and Potomac Pathways. InnerChange's programs then: Optimum Performance Institute (OPI), Chrysalis, New Haven, Sunrise, Vive and Dragonfly Transitions; Lake House and Fulshear were listed under Calo (https://strugglingteens.com/artman/publish/EmbarkBH_BN10312018.shtml). Cressey & Company left in August 2018 (Preqin). OPI and Dragonfly Transitions are young adult programs.

After 2018 "InnerChange" was used as the name of a division of Embark (staff lines on the r/troubledteens wiki), and Sunrise RTC's company is still registered as Solacium Sunrise, LLC.

Rewrite

InnerChange, based in Orem, Utah, owned residential programs for adolescent girls and young women from 2006 to 2018.[1] The private equity firms CIC Partners and Cressey & Company owned it in turn.[2][3] Its core was two Utah treatment centers, New Haven and Sunrise, and it added programs in Texas, Montana, North Carolina and California.[4][5][1][6][7]

Half of the six programs we hold have closed. Alldredge Wilderness Journey in West Virginia closed in 2008. The Oakley School in Utah, which InnerChange began working with in 2013, closed in 2017.[8] Lake House Academy in North Carolina had passed to Calo Programs by 2018 and closed in 2024.

In October 2018 InnerChange, Calo Programs and two other companies took the shared name Embark Behavioral Health. New Haven, Sunrise, Chrysalis, Optimum Performance Institute, Vive and Dragonfly Transitions went into Embark under InnerChange. The InnerChange name lived on as a division of Embark.[9] Sunrise is still registered as Solacium Sunrise, LLC, after the company's first name, Solacium Holdings.[10][9]
