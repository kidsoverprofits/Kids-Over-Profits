Lava Heights Academy is a coed residential treatment center in Toquerville, Utah, run today by Rite of Passage.[1] The Centers for Adolescent Recovery and Education (CARE) built it and opened it in 2012, as a treatment program centered on the performing arts.[2][3] In 2015 it charged $10,500 a month.[1]

In August 2016 CARE sold Lava Heights with its other programs to Sequel Youth and Family Services.[1][4] The sellers later asked a Washington County court to confirm an arbitration award against the Sequel buyers. In July 2019 Sequel closed two of the former CARE programs, Red Rock Canyon School and Mount Pleasant Academy, amid abuse investigations.[5][6] Rite of Passage took over Lava Heights from Sequel in October 2021.[4] Utah has licensed it to Care Youth Corporation, doing business as Rite of Passage, since January 31, 2022.[4]

In June 2018 two boys ran away from Lava Heights and were missing for nearly four days. A mother said staff neither filed a missing-person report nor asked police to publicize the disappearance. A 22-year-old staff member was arrested on several sexual charges after two teenagers reported contact that began in a game of truth or dare.[7] The abuse was alleged to have happened in the facility's bathrooms and dormitory.[7]
