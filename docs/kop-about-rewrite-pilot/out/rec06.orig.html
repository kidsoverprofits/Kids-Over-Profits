Rewrite

The Academy at Swift River was a coed therapeutic boarding school for teenagers in Cummington, Massachusetts, run by Aspen Education Group.[1][1] It opened in 1997, with Tim Brace as its founder and executive director.[2] Its enrollment agreement named the operator as the Academy of Swift River, a Delaware limited liability company. It was the sister school of Mount Bachelor Academy in Oregon and was considered a spinoff of CEDU.[1] It joined the National Association of Therapeutic Schools and Programs (NATSAP) in 1999.[3][4]

Massachusetts did not license it as a private school, alternative education program or special education school.[1] By 2013 it listed approval from the state Department of Education.[3] In 2010 the enrollment agreement charged $6,560 a month plus a $2,500 enrollment fee, for a stay of at least 12 months. It also took adjudicated youth paid for with public funds.

The school was reported to use the same behavior system, called the Positive Control System, as WWASPS programs and Eagle Ranch Academy. A former student alleged that it relied on intense peer pressure, long confrontation sessions and manipulation, which he called brainwashing. Survivors described group sessions in which peers screamed at a student, and physical training handed out as punishment in the wilderness phase. Others have reported forced labor, humiliation, isolation, coercion, physical abuse and verbal attacks meant to humiliate. In 2009 Oregon suspended the license of the sister school, Mount Bachelor, over sleep deprivation, aggressive confrontation groups and role-play, and it closed.[5]

An article dated January 22, 2006, reported that a former Swift River student had died of an overdose. The academy was named in lawsuits in 2008 and 2010, and their outcomes are not known. It closed in 2013. The site is now Swift River Recovery, a program for adults.
