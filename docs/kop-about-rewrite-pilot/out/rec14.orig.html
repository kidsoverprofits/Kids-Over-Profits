Straight – Cincinnati was the Ohio branch of Straight Inc., in Milford, from 1982 to 1987. In 1993 Kids Helping Kids, a program from Hebron, Kentucky, moved into the former Straight building.[1] It later became Pathway Family Center, part of Pathway Family Centers, which bought it.[2] Penny Walker led it as executive director and CEO.[1]

In 2003 a resident of the Kids Helping Kids/Pathway Family Center facility in Cincinnati alleged that staff hit and restrained them. The outcome is not known. In 2007, as Pathway sought to expand into Valparaiso, Indiana, former participants and watchdog groups raised concerns about its confrontational peer-therapy methods.[3] The Milford facility closed for good in autumn 2008 after years of bad publicity and protests. The program said economic realities forced the closure.
