Rewrite

Colorado County Boot Camp was a secure juvenile detention center in Eagle Lake, Texas, that held youth from several counties under a shared contract. Correctional Services Corporation (CSC) opened it in 1998 with 100 beds. It was later called the Colorado County Juvenile Facility.

Youth Services International ran the program from 2001 to 2008. By 2002 it had 156 beds, and CSC owned part of the facility. In late 2004 CSC wrote down the facility's value by $1.5 million. It has since closed.
