Rewrite

Rite of Passage, based in Minden, Nevada, has run residential programs for young people since 1984. We hold records of 28 of its programs in 10 states, and 24 of them are open. Florida has the most, with 11, among them Alachua Academy in Gainesville and Duval Academy in Jacksonville.

Several of its programs came from operators that gave them up.[1] On July 1, 2020, it took over the Arkansas juvenile treatment centers at Dermott, Mansfield and Harrisburg.[1] Their operator, Youth Opportunity Investments, had told the state that April it would stop running them.[1] In 2021 it took over Falcon Ridge Ranch and Lava Heights Academy in Utah from Sequel Youth and Family Services. In March 2021 a Falcon Ridge Ranch staff member was charged with felonies for showing minors in her care explicit photographs of her husband. Prosecutors also alleged she touched the children's buttocks, and she was charged with sexual battery.
