Highlands Youth Academy was a residential juvenile justice facility in Avon Park, Florida, run for the state Department of Juvenile Justice.[1][2] It was also known as Avon Park Youth Academy. G4S ran it under a five-year, $40 million contract with the department.[1]

In July 2015 a grand jury report called for the facility to be closed. The report cited a riot, escapes, run-down buildings and security failures. In August 2017 the Polk County Sheriff's Office arrested three former Highlands staff members on felony charges.[3] The charges included intentionally destroying evidence.[3]

TrueCore Behavioral Solutions later ran the facility.[2] It closed in October 2019, when its contract with the department expired.[2]
