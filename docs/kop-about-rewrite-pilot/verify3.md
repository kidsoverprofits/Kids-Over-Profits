# Verification pass 3: named-person sentences

Scope: sentences that name a specific person together with an accusation, charge, plea, lawsuit allegation, settlement, arbitration, misconduct finding or death. Sentences that refer to someone only by pronoun ("He pleaded guilty...", "She has said...", the Geer "him" sentences) were out of scope and were not checked.
