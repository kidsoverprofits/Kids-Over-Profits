Tony and Betty Argiros founded the Family Foundation School in Hancock, New York, in 1984. It grew out of their foster home and was built on Alcoholics Anonymous's 12 steps. Their son Mike, his wife Cindy and daughter Rita ran it from 1999 until it closed in 2014 as Allynwood Academy. Since New York's Child Victims Act opened a filing window, at least ten civil suits have alleged abuse there. A June 2026 federal suit also names the village and its police.1, 3, 4

Family members and roles

  • Anthony "Tony" Argiros: co-founder; ran the school until 1999. Described in media reports as a recovering gambling addict.1
  • Betty Argiros: co-founder; ran the school until 1999. Described as a recovering alcoholic.1
  • Emmanuel "Mike" Argiros: son; took over in 1999 and owned the school at closing. President of Hancock Partners, a local charity, and on its board since 2006.1
  • Cindy Ray Argiros: Mike's wife; co-ran the school.1
  • Rita Argiros: daughter; executive program director.1

Timeline

  • Early 1980s: Tony and Betty take in teens with addictions as foster parents.
  • 1984: founded the Family Foundation School; it grew to a 158-acre campus.2
  • 1999: Mike Argiros took over, with Cindy and Rita.1
  • Oct. 2013: renamed Allynwood Academy amid a survivor "truth campaign"; the school cited a program change.3
  • Aug. 2014: closed; Mike cited financial problems.3
  • 2019 onward: at least ten Child Victims Act suits alleging physical, emotional and sexual abuse in the 1990s and 2000s.3
  • June 5, 2026: federal suit in Brooklyn seeking about $10M names Mike and Cindy Argiros, related businesses, the Village of Hancock and its police department.4

Allegations

Lawsuits describe strip searches, isolation rooms, restraints, confinement in closets, hard labor and sexual assault by staff. The June 2026 complaint adds digging graves, being forced to eat vomit, and work on the operators' private properties. It alleges local authorities ignored reports because of the family's local influence. In earlier depositions, Mike Argiros denied knowing of abuse.3, 4

Runaways often hid in the Hancock McDonald's bathroom. Survivor accounts also describe clusters of alumni suicides. The family has admitted no wrongdoing.2, 3
