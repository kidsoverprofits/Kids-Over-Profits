Don Vardell is a career program executive who has run at least six programs in five states, most of them for Aspen Education Group. He came from hospital marketing rather than clinical work - a good example of the professional administrator that chains move from program to program. Several of his programs were later sold or closed.1, 2

Roles and timeline

  • Before 2004: senior marketing and business development officer at a tertiary care hospital in Northern Virginia.1
  • Before 2004: administrator of an adolescent RTC in Tennessee, identified by Jared Balmer as Peninsula Village.1, 2
  • 2004-early 2007: executive director, Academy at Swift River (Cummington, MA; Aspen).1
  • Aug. 2007: executive director, Excel Academy (Conroe, TX; Aspen).1, 3
  • Dec. 2008: executive director, Island View RTC, replacing founder Jared Balmer.2
  • Later: executive director of Shortridge Academy (NH) and Mountain Valley Treatment Center (NH), plus the Aspen Institute for Behavioral Assessment (per the network map).4

Programs and what happened to them

  • Academy at Swift River: Aspen closed it in 2013.
  • Island View: sold in 2014 and rebranded as Elevations RTC, now an FHW-affiliated program.2
  • Aspen Institute for Behavioral Assessment: became Viewpoint Center under FHW.
  • Excel Academy, Shortridge Academy: the map marks both closed.4

Connections

  • Aspen Education Group: Bo Turner (senior VP of residential programs, who rehired him in 2007).1
  • Island View founders: Jared Balmer, Kimball DeLaMare, Lorin Broadbent.2
  • Map links: six programs across the Wilderness, Altior, Eckerd and Aspen frames - one of the map's most cross-network people.4
