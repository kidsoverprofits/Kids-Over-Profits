Karr Farnsworth served as president of WWASPS, Robert Lichfield's network of behavior-modification programs, and as director/owner of Cross Creek Programs in La Verkin, Utah. The network map links him to WWASPS (president), Cross Creek (director/owner) and Browning Distance Learning Academy (trustee).1

Roles

  • WWASPS president: the map lists him as president; Ken Kay's profile notes Kay replaced Farnsworth in 1998.1, 2
  • Cross Creek Programs: director/owner. Cross Creek Manor for girls and Cross Creek Center for boys in La Verkin were among the first WWASPS programs.1, 3
  • Browning Distance Learning Academy: trustee.1
  • Earlier: worked at Provo Canyon School before the WWASPS network formed, per the program's history.3

The Wood complaint

The 2013 first amended complaint in Wood v. WWASPS names Farnsworth among WWASPS-linked staff and alleges abuse and fraud across multiple programs. These are the plaintiffs' allegations; the complaint is a pleading, not a judgment, and the outcome is not verified here.4

Connections

  • WWASPS leadership: Robert Lichfield (founder), Ken Kay (later president), Narvin Lichfield, Brent Facer, J. Ralph Atkin.1
  • Cross Creek: founded by Lichfield and Brent Facer; Ron Garrett described in survivor accounts as an administrator.2, 3
