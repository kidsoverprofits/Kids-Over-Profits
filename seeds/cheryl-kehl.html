Cheryl Kehl is a wilderness-therapy clinician who came out of Aspen Achievement Academy and co-founded Second Nature Wilderness. The site's wilderness timeline records her as one of the Aspen alumni who helped define the modern wilderness therapy format.2

Roles and timeline

  • 1990s: clinical director at Aspen Achievement Academy and Aspen Ranch.1
  • 1998: co-founded Second Nature Wilderness with fellow Aspen alumni Devin Glissmeyer and Vaughn Heath; Brad Reedy joined later.2
  • Undated: NATSAP board member; the Brad Reedy profile notes Kehl as NATSAP secretary in 2004.1, 3

Connections

  • Second Nature co-founders: Devin Glissmeyer and Vaughn Heath (Aspen alumni); Brad Reedy (joined later).2
  • Aspen: clinical director roles at Aspen Achievement Academy and Aspen Ranch.1
  • NATSAP: board member; 2004 secretary per the Reedy profile.1, 3
