John Mercer ran Mission Mountain School in Montana and sat on NATSAP's first Board of Directors as treasurer. He is one of the founding-board figures who tied an individual program directly to the industry's trade association at its creation.1, 2

Roles and timeline

  • 1999: member of NATSAP's first Board of Directors, elected treasurer at the first board meeting (the wiki lists him as Mission Mountain School, Montana, 2-year term).1, 2
  • Undated: founder/director of Mission Mountain School.1

Mission Mountain School

Mission Mountain School is recorded on the map as a closed or rebranded NATSAP member program in Montana. Survivor Kathryn Whitehead, who was sent to Mission Mountain, later testified to Congress about her experiences; the advocacy timeline records her testimony alongside Jon Martin Crawford (Family Foundation School) at the 2007 hearing.3

Program-level allegations and outcomes are attributed to Mission Mountain School; the map and wiki do not tie a specific incident to Mercer personally. Verify his exact role, dates and any direct involvement before describing personal responsibility.
