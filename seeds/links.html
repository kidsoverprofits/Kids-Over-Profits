Lawsuit: Fuller vs. Hyde School 2025

Class Action Complaint for Child Labor Trafficking and Abuse: Jessica Fuller, a former student, alleges that Hyde School operated as a cult-like system of forced labor and psychological abuse disguised as “character education.” She claims students were emotionally and physically abused, denied medical care, and forced into unpaid labor to benefit school leadership.

‘I’m Gay! I’m Gay! I’m Gay! I’m a Homosexual!’: Overt and Covert Conversion Therapy Practices in Therapeutic Boarding Schools by Sarah Golightley, The British Journal of Social Work, 2023

This survivor-led study examines how LGBTQ+ youth experienced conversion therapy inside U.S. therapeutic boarding schools, a branch of the Troubled Teen Industry. Through interviews with former students, it documents how identity was pathologized and suppressed under the label of treatment. The study focuses on both overt and subtle tactics used to enforce conformity, resulting in long-term psychological harm. It calls on social workers to recognize how conversion practices can appear in evolving and often unspoken ways.
