Kent Tasso is the founder and owner of CERTS (Certified Educational, Recreational, and Therapeutic Schools and Programs), a Utah company. The KOP operator history and CERTS's own materials record the programs he built for teenage girls.1, 2

Roles and programs

  • 2001: founded CERTS, based in Murray, Utah (the wiki dates the group to 2001; CERTS's own blog dates his first program to 2002).1, 3
  • 2002: opened Kolob Canyon RTC in New Harmony.1
  • 2003: opened Moonridge Academy in Cedar City.1
  • 2005: opened La Europa Academy in Murray.1
  • 2004: bought the Academy at Cedar Mountain (opened 1999) and renamed it Mountain Springs Preparatory Academy.1

In a notice printed in Woodbury Reports (issue #147, p. 29), Tasso, described as owner and founder of CERTS, announced that Craig Rodabough would become the company's executive director and oversee La Europa Academy, Kolob Canyon RTC, Moonridge and Mountain Springs.2

Program-level accounts

Former residents quoted on the r/troubledteens wiki allege communication restrictions, forced manual labor and medical neglect at Kolob Canyon, and describe "accountability" groups at La Europa Academy in which staff and other girls criticized one girl seated in the middle of a circle. The wiki notes that these reports have not been investigated. KOP's glossary records rules used at Moonridge Academy, among them "No talk," a ban on conversation.1

As of 2026 the CERTS website lists four programs (Kolob Canyon, La Europa Academy, Moonridge Academy and Mountain Springs Preparatory Academy) and displays Joint Commission accreditation.1, 4
