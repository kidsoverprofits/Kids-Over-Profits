Shayne and Sheri Gallagher are a married couple who co-founded WinGate Wilderness Therapy in Kanab, Utah, around 2008 and grew it with backing from Family Help & Wellness. WinGate closed in September 2023. They matter less as public figures than as long-serving wilderness operators whose careers reach back to the field's early programs.1, 3

Roles and timeline

  • ~1990 onward: Shayne - field staff, trainer, program developer and executive director at several wilderness programs. Sheri - field staff at "one of the original wilderness therapy programs," where she met Shayne; later worked at four other wilderness programs.2
  • Before WinGate: Sheri was special projects manager at Outback Therapeutic Expeditions (per the network map). Shayne's pre-WinGate program has not been identified.
  • ~2008: co-founded WinGate; Shayne as executive director, Sheri as director of operations.1
  • 2012: Shayne also listed as executive director of "WinGate & Waypoints Wilderness Therapy Programs."
  • Sept. 2023: WinGate closed.3

Background claims worth checking

Sheri's WinGate bio says that as part of her BYU master's in Recreation Administration, she helped design and run "the 1st long-term wilderness therapy program." If true, that ties her to the BYU-born lineage of Larry Dean Olsen and Anasazi - the same roots as Aspen Achievement Academy and the Utah wilderness industry.2

Shayne says his own high school experience led him into wilderness therapy. Check whether he was a participant in an early program.1

WinGate and Family Help & Wellness

WinGate marketed itself as "owner-operated," but FHW backed it and listed it among its managed programs. The 2023 closure podcast describes WinGate's rise "under Shayne and Sherry Gallagher and Family Help and Wellness." WinGate closed in a wave alongside SUWS of the Carolinas, Aspiro and Outback.3

WinGate staff later turned up at other programs. Drive notes list Josh Gunalda as admissions director at both WinGate and SUWS of the Carolinas. FHW's 2021 program list in Oregon testimony includes WinGate.
