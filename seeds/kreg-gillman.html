Kreg Gillman is a therapist turned executive who links more networks on the network map than anyone else: 7 programs across 6 frames. He ran Aspen Ranch and SunHawk Academy for Aspen, then Provo Canyon School for UHS, and co-founded Silverado with Provo Canyon's founder - showing how the same leadership circulated between Aspen, UHS and independent Utah programs.1, 4

Roles and timeline

  • 1993: therapist at Provo Canyon School.1
  • 1996: director of admissions at Provo Canyon School.1
  • 1998: director of admissions for Aspen Achievement Academy and Aspen Ranch (Aspen Education Group).1
  • ~1998-2001: executive director of Aspen Ranch for three years.1
  • ~2001-2003: executive director of SunHawk Academy (St. George, UT), at Aspen's request.1
  • Jan. 2004: returned to Provo Canyon School as CEO, under UHS.1
  • ~2006: helped Provo Canyon co-founder Robert Crist start Silverado; the map lists him as executive director of Silverado Boys Ranch.2, 4
  • Later: CEO of Salt Lake Behavioral Health (UHS).2

The map also links him to RedCliff Ascent (clinical director) and WayPoint Academy (executive director); neither role appears in the sources found so far.4

Programs he led and what happened to them

  • Provo Canyon School: abuse and overmedication allegations stretch back to its 1971 founding. Utah revoked the license for its main Provo campus on July 17, 2026, and UHS closed the school. Gillman's CEO years were 2004 to about 2006 - well before the closure.3
  • Silverado: closed in 2016 after reported sexual abuse, per Breaking Code Silence.2
  • Aspen Ranch: an Aspen program in Loa, Utah, run on a six-level system like Provo Canyon's. It later closed.1

Connections

  • Robert Crist: Provo Canyon co-founder and Silverado partner.
  • Ken Huey and Brad Gerrard: Gillman visited Woodbury Reports with them in fall 2003.1
  • Industry media: his 2004 Woodbury Reports profile ran as a paid advertisement.1
  • Map links: Provo Canyon School and UHS (CEO); Aspen Ranch, SunHawk Academy, WayPoint Academy and Silverado Boys Ranch (executive director); RedCliff Ascent (clinical director).4
