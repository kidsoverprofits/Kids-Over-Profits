Bill Lane was a Synanon member and president of CEDU High School, and owned Bill Lane & Associates, a transport/escort company serving the CEDU family of programs. The map records Bill Lane & Associates as a transportation service provider to CEDU Family of Services.1, 2

Roles

  • Synanon: member.1
  • CEDU High School: president.1
  • Bill Lane & Associates: owner; a transport/escort company. The years-research data quotes that "since 1992, Bill has been helping families begin treatment respectfully, transporting and managing a transport company."1, 2

Why he matters

Transport companies are the link between a family's home and a program: they take children, sometimes by force, to the facility. Lane's position - a CEDU High School president running the transport company that served the CEDU programs - puts the program and the transport in the same hands. This is the pattern the site's youth-transport-company research tracks.2

Connections

  • Map links: Synanon (member), CEDU High School (president), Bill Lane & Associates (owner); Bill Lane & Associates to CEDU Family of Services (transportation service provider).1
  • CEDU lineage: Mel Wasserman (CEDU founder); the Synanon-to-CEDU line runs through Chuck Dederich's profile.
  • Not the same person: Bill Wilson, co-founder of Alcoholics Anonymous, is a separate, earlier figure (Oxford Group member; founded AA in 1935).3
