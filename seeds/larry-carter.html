Larry Carter spent 13 years at Provo Canyon School before co-founding Logan River Academy in 2000 with fellow Provo Canyon alumni, and later served as a senior vice president of inpatient services at Aspen Education Group.1, 2

Roles and timeline

  • 13 years: therapist at Provo Canyon School.1, 2
  • 2000: co-founded Logan River Academy with Jeff Smith; Robert Crist (Provo Canyon's co-founder) became its medical director.1, 2
  • Later: senior vice president of inpatient services at Aspen Education Group.2

Logan River Academy

In 2019 a former resident sued Logan River Academy, alleging repeated sexual abuse by an employee. The suit is an allegation; the facility record and the Robert Crist profile note it without a verified outcome.2

Connections
