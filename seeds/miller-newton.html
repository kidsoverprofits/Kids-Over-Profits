Virgil Miller Newton is an anthropologist who was national clinical director of Straight Inc. and left amid allegations of abuse and insurance fraud. He founded KIDS Centers of America, a chain of adolescent drug treatment programs, opening KIDS of Bergen County in Hackensack, New Jersey, in 1984.1, 2

Roles and timeline

  • Before 1984: national clinical director of Straight Inc.; left amid allegations of abuse and insurance fraud.1
  • 1984: opened the first KIDS center, KIDS of Bergen County (Hackensack, NJ), the chain's headquarters, at its height holding more than 175 young people.1
  • 1986-1990: opened KIDS of El Paso (1986), KIDS of Greater Salt Lake and KIDS of Southern California in Yorba Linda (1988), and KIDS of the Canadian West in Calgary (1990).1
  • 1990: in a civil suit against Straight and its clinical director Miller Newton, the case ended in a $720,000 award.3
  • 1998: a survivor of Newton's KIDS program sued Newton, KIDS of North Jersey and their psychiatrists for abuse, and was awarded $4.5 million.3
  • Nov. 1998: KIDS of North Jersey closed after New Jersey cut off its Medicaid payments, citing its use of physical restraints and of patients as staff.1, 2

The KIDS model and its collapse

The program copied Straight's: newcomers lived in the homes of older participants and spent most of each day in group sessions run by peer counselors. Newton planned a national chain of 10 to 25 centers.1

The centers outside New Jersey closed within a few years. Texas's attorney general obtained a court order in 1988 to close KIDS of El Paso after state investigations of abuse complaints; California told KIDS of Southern California in May 1988 that it was operating without a license, and it closed in 1989. In Utah, two peer counselors were charged with assault and unlawful detention in January 1990, two lawsuits alleged false imprisonment, and the state suspended the program's license in June 1990. Former participants later won large settlements, including $6.5 million paid by the program's insurers in 2003.1, 2, 3

Connections

  • Straight lineage: Straight Inc. (Mel and Betty Sembler) grew out of Art Barker's Seed; KIDS was Newton's Straight offshoot. KIDS of Greater Salt Lake was later directed by Kimball DeLaMare.1
