Mel Sembler co-founded Straight, Inc. with Betty Sembler; the archived Straight history places its founding in 1976 and also names Joseph Zappala. The 2002 Sembler Report says Mel and/or Betty served on Straight's board from 1976 to 2002 and quotes Mel describing himself as its board president for 17 years. This report is an advocacy document, so distinguish its account from the primary corporate records and Senate transcript it cites.1, 5

The Sembler Report also describes Mel Sembler's Republican fundraising and diplomatic appointments, including Australia, and reproduces his 2001 Senate confirmation statement that he and Betty helped found Straight and that he chaired its board for 17 years. Verify appointments against State Department records and testimony against the official Senate hearing transcript before quoting it as independently established fact.5, 6

The Sembler Report

The International Survivors Action Committee (ISAC Corporation) published The Sembler Report on December 9, 2002. It says it compiles a timeline of Mel and Betty Sembler's relationship to Straight and alleged abuse, and thanks researcher Wes Fager. Treat its conclusions as the report authors' claims; verify material allegations against the listed newspapers, state records, court files and the Senate transcript.5

  • The report says a February 14, 1978 Florida HRS letter to Mel Sembler warned that force must not be used to stop a young person leaving Straight. The report cites the letter; obtain the original agency record to verify context and exact wording.5, 7
  • The report alleges that state health officials in 1993 investigated claims Mel interfered with licensing inspectors and records allegations around Straight's St. Petersburg closure. These are claims in the report, not a finding against Sembler; consult the underlying HRS report and contemporaneous coverage.5
  • The report questions the qualifications of some Straight executives and asserts that Sembler approved or hired them. Attribute that conclusion to ISAC and verify each person's role and credentials independently.5
