Tim Brace is a program director and headmaster who moved through the CEDU lineage and into Aspen, and was NATSAP's first vice president. The NATSAP wiki records that he began his career at CEDU High School in 1980 and went on to found the Academy at Swift River.2

Roles and timeline

  • 1980: began at CEDU High School, working consecutively as counselor, family head, assistant director and director of CEDU's Rocky Mountain Academy (Bonners Ferry, ID), then returned to CEDU High School as headmaster.2
  • 1993: left CEDU to become executive director/headmaster of Mount Bachelor Academy, a CEDU spin-off and among the first Aspen Education Group schools.2
  • 1997: created the Academy at Swift River, another Aspen Education Group program.2
  • 1999: on NATSAP's first Board of Directors, elected vice president.2
  • Undated: executive director of educational services for Aspen Education Group; headmaster at Carlbrook School.1, 2

Programs and their histories

The programs Brace led carry documented program-level concerns; the map does not tie a specific incident to him personally.

  • Rocky Mountain Academy: a CEDU sister school; closed in 2005. Lon Woodbury was its admissions director before founding the Woodbury Reports.1
  • Mount Bachelor Academy: a CEDU spin-off; Oregon DHS found nine confirmed abuse allegations in 2009 and ordered its license suspended. Three lawsuits were filed in 2011-2012.2
  • Academy at Swift River: Aspen closed it in 2013.1
