Graham Shannonhouse founded Trails Carolina, the Family Help & Wellness wilderness program in Lake Toxaway, North Carolina, and served as its executive director. The network map links him to Trails Carolina as founder/executive director; earlier he was a program director at SUWS of Idaho.1, 2

Roles

  • SUWS of Idaho: program director (earlier role).2
  • Trails Carolina: founder and executive director.1

Trails Carolina

These are program-level events attributed to Trails Carolina and its owner, Family Help & Wellness, not to Shannonhouse personally.

  • 2014: Alec Lansing, 17, died of hypothermia after running away from Trails Carolina.3
  • Feb. 3, 2024: Clark Harman, 12, died at Trails Carolina a day after he arrived; the medical examiner ruled the death, by asphyxia in a sleeping bag, a homicide; the district attorney declined to file charges. North Carolina removed every child, fined the program $18,000 and revoked its license on May 17, 2024.3
  • 2024-2026: federal lawsuits allege abuse, neglect and forced labor at Trails Carolina, Asheville Academy and Solstice East.3
